Privacy policy

Short version: Sealcloak collects nothing. Your files stay on your phone, encrypted.

What we collect

Nothing. Sealcloak has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not have Android’s internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

Your files

Photos, videos, documents, audio, notes and scans you add are encrypted on your phone and stored only in the app’s private storage. We never receive them and cannot open them.

Permissions the app uses

  • Camera: for the document scanner and, if you turn it on, intruder photos. Pictures are saved encrypted in your vault only.
  • Fingerprint: to unlock, if you turn it on. Android handles your fingerprint; the app never sees it.
  • Run at startup: so scheduled backups keep working after a restart.

Backups

Backup files are created only when you ask, or on a schedule you set, and saved to a folder you choose. They are encrypted. Where they go after that is up to you.

Purchases

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google’s privacy policy. The app only learns whether Pro is active; it never receives your payment details.

This website

This site has no analytics or cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address when your browser fetches them.

Children

Sealcloak is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
