Questions

About paying

What happens if my subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. All your files stay and keep working. You just can’t add more beyond the free limits until you renew.

How does the free trial work?

The annual plan starts with 7 free days for new subscribers. Cancel in Google Play before it ends and you won’t be charged.

Do I need an account to buy Pro?

No Sealcloak account. Google Play handles the payment with the Google account already on your phone. Use “Restore purchases” in the app on a new phone.
