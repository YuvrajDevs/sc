Remove location from photos

    Quick answer

    To remove the location from a photo, open it in the tool above and download the clean copy. It removes the GPS coordinates, phone model, date and other hidden data without touching the image. To stop new photos getting a location, turn off location tags in your camera app’s settings.

    What a photo can reveal

    Photos store extra details called EXIF metadata. Depending on your phone and settings, a single picture can include:

    • GPS coordinates, often accurate to a few metres. A photo taken at home can reveal your address.
    • Date and time to the second.
    • Phone make and model, and sometimes the lens and camera settings.
    • Editing history and text notes added by some apps.

    None of this is visible when you look at the photo. It travels with the file whenever you send it as a document, upload it to a website, post it on a forum, or share it by email, Bluetooth or a cloud link.

    How to stop your Android phone adding location

    1. Open your Camera app and go to its settings (usually a gear icon).
    2. Find Location tags, Save location or GPS tag and turn it off.
    3. Take a test photo and check it here: no location should appear.

    The wording differs by brand (Samsung, Xiaomi, vivo, OPPO and Pixel each name it slightly differently). Photos you already took still carry their location, so clean those before sharing.

    How this tool works

    The tool reads the photo inside your browser, removes the blocks that hold hidden data (EXIF and XMP, IPTC and comments in JPEGs, and text chunks in PNGs), and gives you a new file. The image data itself is copied exactly, so there’s no quality loss. Because phones store rotation in the same place, it writes back the rotation alone, so your photo stays the right way up.

    There’s no upload, no account and no tracking on this site. You can even load the page, switch off your internet, and it still works.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

    No. The tool runs entirely in your browser tab. Your photo is read, cleaned and handed back on your own device, and the page makes no network requests with it.

    Does removing the location lower the photo quality?

    No. The tool removes the hidden data blocks only. The image itself is copied byte for byte, so nothing is re-compressed.

    Will my photo turn sideways after cleaning?

    It shouldn't. Phones store rotation in the same hidden data, so the tool writes back just the rotation and nothing else.

    Do WhatsApp and Instagram remove location already?

    Most big apps strip location when they compress a photo, but files sent as documents, by email, Bluetooth, cloud links or USB usually keep everything. Cleaning first means you don't have to rely on it.

    Can I stop my phone adding location in the first place?

    Yes. In your camera app's settings, turn off location tags (often called Save location or Location tags). New photos then won't include GPS coordinates.
