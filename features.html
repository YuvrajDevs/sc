Features

Everything you need to keep things private.

Nothing you don’t. Every feature works offline, on your phone.

Locking

A lock that’s yours alone.

  • 5×5 pattern. Far more combinations than a 3×3 phone lock.
  • Fingerprint unlock. Optional, after you’ve drawn your pattern once.
  • Intruder photos. A wrong pattern quietly snaps the front camera.
  • Locks itself when you leave the app.
Vaults

Separate vaults for separate things.

Each vault has its own pattern and its own recovery phrase. Drawing one pattern opens only that vault. Nothing on screen says the others exist.

  • Three vaults on the free plan, unlimited with Pro
  • Folders inside each vault
  • Search everything in the open vault

Stay unseen

Disguise as another app PRO

Give Sealcloak any picture and name, so on your home screen it looks like an ordinary app.

Opens to your pattern PRO

Coerce

A harmless vault with its own pattern. If you’re forced to unlock, draw it and show there’s nothing to see.

No screenshots

Erase after wrong tries

Off by default. Turn it on and too many wrong patterns erase every vault. You must type PERMIT to enable it.

Out of the gallery

Inside the vault

More than a photo locker.

Photos & videos

View and play them without ever saving an unencrypted copy.

Documents & audio

PDFs, files and recordings, all encrypted.

Private notes

Write notes that live only in the vault.

Document scanner

Scan papers and IDs into the vault as a PDF. The pages are saved to the vault, not your gallery.

Never lose a file

Backups without a cloud.

Recovery phrase

Nine random words, or your own sentence of six words or more. It opens the vault if you forget the pattern.

Encrypted backup file

Scheduled backup

In detail

How each feature works.

pattern lock for photos

Pattern lock for photos: a 5×5 grid only you know

Its own 5×5 pattern, separate from your phone's lock, with optional fingerprint.
encrypt photos

How Sealcloak encrypts your photos and files

AES-256-GCM on the phone, a random key per vault, unlocked by your pattern.
multiple hidden vaults

Hidden vaults: separate vaults, each with its own pattern

Separate vaults with their own patterns. Nothing shows how many exist.
decoy vault

Coerce: a decoy vault for when you're forced to unlock

A harmless decoy vault with its own pattern, for when someone makes you unlock.
calculator vault

Calculator vault: hide Sealcloak as a working calculator

A working calculator on your home screen. Your secret code opens the vault.
disguise app icon

Custom icon: disguise Sealcloak as any app you like

Your own picture and name on the home screen. Opens straight to your pattern.
intruder photos

Intruder photos: see who tried to open your vault

From the 3rd wrong pattern in a row, the front camera photographs each attempt.
vault recovery phrase

Vault recovery phrase: get back in if you forget your pattern

9 words that get you back in. Without them, nobody can open the vault.
photo vault backup

Encrypted backups: move your vault to a new phone

One encrypted file, by hand or on a schedule. Restore on any phone.
private camera app

Private camera and document scanner

Capture photos, videos and scans straight into the vault.
self destruct vault

Erase after wrong patterns: an optional self-destruct

Optional: erase everything after too many wrong patterns. Off by default.
share photos securely

Share files securely as a passphrase-locked package

One passphrase-locked file. Send it with any app, and the passphrase separately.
