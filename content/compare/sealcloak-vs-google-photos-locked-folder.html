Sealcloak vs Google Photos Locked Folder: when you need more than a locked folder

Google Photos' Locked Folder is already on most Android phones, and it's a sensible first step: photos you move there vanish from your grid, albums, search and memories. But it opens with the same screen lock as your phone, and it only holds photos and videos. If the people you're worried about know your screen lock, you need a vault with its own key.

Quick answer

Choose Sealcloak when Locked Folder isn't enough. Locked Folder opens with your phone's screen lock, so anyone who knows it can get in. Sealcloak has its own pattern, encrypts every file with AES-256-GCM using a key made from that pattern, holds documents and audio too, and adds hidden vaults, a decoy and intruder photos, all offline.

The screen-lock problem

Locked Folder's protection is your phone's screen lock. That's convenient: no extra code to remember. It's also its biggest limit. The people most likely to look through your photos are the people closest to you, and they often know your screen lock already, or have watched you unlock your phone a hundred times. A partner, a parent, a sibling, a friend who borrowed the phone: for all of them, Locked Folder is one more tap away.

Sealcloak's vault has its own pattern on a 5×5 grid, unrelated to your screen lock. Even with the phone unlocked in someone else's hands, the vault stays shut. And because the pattern is turned into the encryption key with Argon2id, the files are unreadable without it, not just hidden.

Not just photos

Private things on a phone aren't only photos. There are passport and ID scans, medical letters, voice notes, bank statements and things you write down. Locked Folder holds photos and videos inside Google Photos. Sealcloak holds all of them, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Its document scanner turns paper into a PDF inside the vault, and the private camera saves new photos straight into it.

Built for pressure, not just privacy

Locked Folder has one folder and one way in. Sealcloak is designed for the moments when someone is actively trying to see inside. Up to three vaults can exist, each with its own pattern, and none reveals the others. Coerce opens a harmless decoy vault when you're pushed to unlock. Intruder photos show who tried guessing. The calculator disguise means there's no "Photos" app to point at in the first place.

Online backup or offline backup

Google Photos can back up Locked Folder items to your Google account, which protects against losing your phone but puts a copy online, linked to your account. Sealcloak has no internet permission and no account, so nothing goes online. Instead, it makes encrypted backup files by hand or on a weekly or monthly schedule, saved where you choose. Only your recovery phrase or the passphrase you set can open them.

When Locked Folder is enough

If you just want a few photos out of your main gallery and nobody around you knows your screen lock, Locked Folder is a reasonable start, and our guide on hiding photos on Android explains how to use it. The moment the risk is someone who knows your phone, or the files include documents, a separate encrypted vault is the safer place.

Who outgrows Locked Folder

People usually move on from Locked Folder after a specific moment: a partner who knows the screen lock, a child who unlocks the phone with a watched pattern, a phone handed over at a repair shop, or a scan of a passport that doesn't belong in Google Photos at all. In each case, the fix is the same: a separate key that nobody else has seen, and files that stay unreadable without it.

Moving from Locked Folder

  1. Install Sealcloak, draw a pattern that's different from your screen lock, and write down the recovery phrase.
  2. In Google Photos, open Locked Folder, select the items and move them out.
  3. Add them to Sealcloak and check a few photos and videos open properly.
  4. Delete them from Google Photos, then empty Google Photos' trash.
  5. If Locked Folder backup was on, make sure the copies are gone from your Google account too.
  6. Set up a scheduled encrypted backup in Sealcloak.

Bottom line

Locked Folder hides photos behind your screen lock. Sealcloak protects all your private files behind a separate pattern that becomes the encryption key, with hidden vaults, a decoy, intruder photos and offline backups only you can open.
