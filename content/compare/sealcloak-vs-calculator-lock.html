Sealcloak vs Calculator Lock: a calculator lock that's truly private

Calculator Lock - Hide Photos has more than 50 million installs and the classic trick: a calculator on the outside, hidden photos on the inside, plus online storage shared between your devices. Sealcloak can be a working calculator too. The difference is what's behind the keypad: an encrypted vault that never touches the internet.

Quick answer

Sealcloak is the more private Calculator Lock alternative. Its calculator opens a vault where every file is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key from your pattern, with no internet permission, no online storage and no data shared. Calculator Lock's data safety section lists device IDs shared for advertising and says data can't be deleted on request.

Two locks, not one

With many calculator vaults, the passcode typed on the keypad is the only thing standing between someone and your photos. Anyone who sees you type it once has everything. Sealcloak splits that in two. Your secret code of 4 to 8 digits, followed by holding the equals key for two seconds, only reveals the door. Your pattern, or fingerprint if you've turned it on, opens the vault. And that pattern isn't just checked; it's turned into the encryption key with Argon2id.

Behind the keypad: encryption, not hiding

Calculator Lock's listing describes hiding photos and videos and storing files in online storage, but not how the files on your phone are encrypted. Sealcloak encrypts every file with AES-256-GCM as it's added. If someone finds the app's storage, copies it or connects the phone to a computer, they see unreadable data. Each vault has its own random master key, protected with help from the Android Keystore.

Online storage you can't delete

Syncing your hidden photos across devices means they live on a server. Calculator Lock's Play data safety section also states that data can't be deleted on request, and lists device IDs shared for analytics and advertising. Once private photos are on a server you don't control, you can't be sure where they are or when they're truly gone.

Sealcloak keeps no online copy of anything, because it can't: it has no internet permission. Your protection against losing the phone is an encrypted backup that you make by hand or on a weekly or monthly schedule, saved in a folder you choose and openable only with your recovery phrase or passphrase.

More than one disguise

Calculator vaults are so common that some people know to look for a second calculator. Sealcloak offers an alternative: a custom icon with any picture and name, so it can blend in as anything on your home screen. Whichever you choose, screenshots inside the app are blocked, so the recent apps screen shows a blank card instead of your photos.

When someone won't take no for an answer

A disguise helps until someone knows about it. Sealcloak's Coerce gives you a separate decoy vault with its own pattern: if you're made to open the app, open that one. Up to three vaults can exist without any sign of how many. Intruder photos record who tried guessing, from the third wrong pattern in a row. And for people at serious risk, erase after wrong patterns is available, off by default and only switched on by typing PERMIT.

Life inside the vault

Once you're in, Sealcloak is a full private gallery. Folders keep things organised, search finds files by name, and the viewer plays photos as a slideshow and videos in full screen. It isn't only for pictures: PDFs and other documents, audio recordings and text notes are encrypted the same way. The private camera and document scanner capture straight into the vault, and secure sharing sends files as a passphrase-locked package when you do need to share.

Switching

  1. Install Sealcloak, draw a pattern of six or more dots and write down the recovery phrase.
  2. In Calculator Lock, unhide or export your photos, videos and documents to the gallery.
  3. Add them to Sealcloak and open a few to check.
  4. Delete the gallery copies, empty the trash, and remove your files from Calculator Lock's online storage.
  5. In Sealcloak's settings, turn on the calculator disguise and choose your secret code.
