How it works

From install to hidden in a minute.

Draw your pattern

Connect at least six dots on the 5×5 grid. Draw it twice to confirm.

Save your phrase

Write the nine words on paper, or use a sentence of your own. Keep them away from the phone.

Add files

Tap + and pick photos, videos, documents or audio. Choose to remove the originals from the gallery.

Every day

Open, look, lock.

Draw your pattern or use your fingerprint. Tap Lock now, or just leave the app and it locks itself.

draw pattern → vault opens
leave app → vault locks
wrong pattern → intruder photo
Forgot your pattern

Your phrase gets you back in.

Tap “Use recovery phrase”, type it, then draw a new pattern. Your old pattern stops working.

When you’re ready

Go further.

Add another vault

Settings → More vaults. Draw a new pattern. It opens a vault of its own.

Make a backup

Settings → Backup. Export once, or schedule weekly or monthly backups to a folder.

Hide the app

Settings → Calculator mode (Pro). Choose a code, test it, and the icon becomes a calculator.

New phone

Moving to a new phone.

Export

On the old phone, export a backup of each vault.

Copy

Move the backup files over with a cable, a USB stick or any app.

Import

Install Sealcloak, choose Import a backup, and type that vault’s recovery phrase.
