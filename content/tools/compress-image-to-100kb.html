Compress image to 100 KB

Many application forms only accept photos under 100 KB. Drop your photo in, and download a copy that fits, with the best quality possible at that size.

Quick answer

To compress an image to 100 KB, choose it above and tap Download: the target is already set to 100 KB. The tool keeps the highest quality that fits and only reduces the dimensions if needed. Nothing is uploaded.

How to compress a photo to 100 KB

  1. Choose your photo above, or drop it on the box. You can do several at once.
  2. Check the target size. It's set to 100 KB; type another number if your form asks for something different.
  3. Keep JPEG as the format: it's accepted everywhere. Pick WebP only for websites.
  4. Tap Download next to the result. The file name ends in its new size so you can find it.

How it works

JPEG and WebP photos have a quality setting. The tool tries different settings and keeps the highest quality that still fits your target, so the photo looks as good as it can at that size. If even a low quality is too big (a huge photo squeezed into a tiny limit), it shrinks the width and height step by step until it fits. You'll see the new size next to each photo before you download it.

Where you'll need it

  • Government, passport and visa application portals.
  • College admission and scholarship forms.
  • Job portals and HR systems that cap photo uploads.
  • Profile pictures on sites with strict limits.

Some forms ask for a range, such as 20 to 100 KB. Aim a little under the maximum, for example 90 KB, so the file is safely accepted.

Private by design

The tool runs entirely inside this browser tab. Your photo is opened, edited and saved on your own device, and it is never sent to a server, so there is nothing for anyone to keep, scan or leak. No account, no watermark and no limit on how many photos you do. It even keeps working if you switch off your internet after the page has loaded.

Tips for the sharpest result

  • Crop first. Cutting away empty background leaves more of the size budget for the part that matters. Use the crop tool, then compress.
  • Set the longest side if the form also lists pixel dimensions. Smaller dimensions at good quality usually look better than big dimensions at low quality.
  • Screenshots and scans of text compress very well as JPEG once they're cropped to the page.

Photos you never want anyone to see are better kept out of the gallery altogether. See how to hide photos on Android, or how Sealcloak keeps them encrypted on your phone.
