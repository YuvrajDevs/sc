PNG to WebP converter

Turn PNG files into WebP in a couple of taps. Drop in one file or a whole batch; each one converts on your device with its own download button.

Quick answer

To convert PNG to WebP, choose your PNG files above and tap Download next to each result. The conversion runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded and there's no limit on how many files you convert.

How to convert PNG to WebP

  1. Choose or drop your PNG files above. You can select many at once.
  2. Each file converts straight away and shows its new size.
  3. Tap Download next to each one.

Why convert PNG to WebP

WebP files are much smaller than PNG and JPG at the same visual quality, so websites load faster and use less data. Every modern browser shows WebP. Use it for website images, blogs and online shops. For sending photos to people, JPG is still the safest choice because every app opens it.
