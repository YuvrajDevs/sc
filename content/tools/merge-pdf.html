Merge PDF

Join several PDFs into a single document: statements, scans, forms and attachments. Put them in order, merge, done. Nothing is uploaded.

Quick answer

To merge PDF files, choose two or more PDFs above, use the arrows to set the order, and tap Merge PDFs. You get one PDF with every page, in that order. The merge happens on your device, so your documents are never uploaded.

How to merge PDFs

  1. Choose or drop two or more PDF files.
  2. Use ↑ and ↓ to put them in order, and × to remove one.
  3. Tap Merge PDFs and download merged.pdf.

Good to know

  • Pages keep their original size, orientation and quality; nothing is re-compressed.
  • Password-protected PDFs can't be merged until the password is removed in the app that created them.
  • To merge photos rather than PDFs, use JPG to PDF. To drop pages first, use delete PDF pages.

Private by design

Most PDF websites upload your document to their servers to process it. This one doesn't. The PDF is opened, edited and saved inside this browser tab, on your own device, using open-source PDF libraries the page loads. Contracts, bank statements, ID scans and medical letters never leave your phone or computer. No sign-up, no watermark, no file limit.

Merged documents often contain personal details. Keep them in an encrypted place afterwards; Sealcloak's vault keeps PDFs out of your Downloads folder and cloud.
