Share your Wi-Fi password from Android

Android can show your Wi-Fi password as a QR code so a friend can scan and join. For guests who visit often, make a printable code instead.

Quick answer

On Android 10 and newer, open Settings → Network & internet (or Connections) → Wi-Fi, tap your network or its gear icon, then Share or QR code. Unlock to show the code; your friend scans it with their camera. For a printed sign, make a code with the tool above.

Using Android's built-in QR

  1. Open Settings and go to Wi-Fi (Network & internet on Pixel, Connections on Samsung).
  2. Tap the network you're connected to, or its gear icon.
  3. Tap Share or QR code, and confirm with your fingerprint or PIN.
  4. Your friend points their camera at the code and taps Join. Many phones also show the password in text under the code.

A printable code for guests

  1. Type the network name and password in the tool above, and choose WPA.
  2. Download the PNG and print it, or save the SVG for a sign.
  3. Test it with your phone camera before putting it up.

Keep it safe

Anyone who scans the code can join, so use a guest network if your router supports it. If you change your Wi-Fi password, make a new code. Codes made here are generated on your device; the password isn't sent anywhere. For stronger passwords, try our password generator.

Private by design

The Wi-Fi QR code tool on this page runs entirely inside your browser tab. Your files are opened, processed and saved on your own device and are never sent to a server, so there's nothing for anyone to store, scan or leak. There's no sign-up, no watermark and no limit, and once the page has loaded it keeps working even if you switch off your internet.
