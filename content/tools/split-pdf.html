Split PDF

Pull out just the pages you need, like one bank statement page or a single signed form, or split a PDF into separate one-page files.

Quick answer

To split a PDF, choose it above, type the pages you want (for example 1-3, 5), choose whether to keep them in one PDF or save each page separately, and tap Split PDF. The PDF is split on your device and never uploaded.

How to split a PDF

  1. Choose the PDF. You'll see how many pages it has.
  2. Type pages and ranges, such as 2, 1-3, 7 or 5- for page 5 to the end. Leave it blank for all pages.
  3. Choose Selected pages in one PDF or Every page as its own PDF.
  4. Tap Split PDF and download the results.

When it's useful

  • Sending only the relevant page of a long statement or contract.
  • Uploading a single page to a form that limits file size.
  • Sharing part of a document without exposing the rest of your personal details.

Private by design

Most PDF websites upload your document to their servers to process it. This one doesn't. The PDF is opened, edited and saved inside this browser tab, on your own device, using open-source PDF libraries the page loads. Contracts, bank statements, ID scans and medical letters never leave your phone or computer. No sign-up, no watermark, no file limit.

Need the opposite? Merge PDFs into one. Want pages as images instead? Try PDF to JPG.
