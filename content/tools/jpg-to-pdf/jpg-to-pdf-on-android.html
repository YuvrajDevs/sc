JPG to PDF on Android

Need to send photos of documents as a single PDF from your phone? You don't need to install an app or upload anything. Do it right here in your browser.

Quick answer

On your Android phone, choose the photos in the tool above, order them with the arrows, pick A4 or Letter and tap Create PDF. Download it and share it from your Downloads folder. The PDF is made on your phone.

Step by step on Android

  1. Tap Choose photos above and select them from your gallery or Files.
  2. Use ↑ and ↓ to put the pages in order.
  3. Choose A4 (most countries) or Letter (US, Canada), or Fit to image.
  4. Tap Create PDF, then Download. It's saved to your Downloads folder.
  5. Share it from Files or attach it to email or WhatsApp as a document.

Better document photos

  • Lay the page flat in good, even light; avoid flash glare.
  • Hold the phone directly above, parallel to the page.
  • Crop away the table or background first.
  • If the PDF must be small, compress the photos first.
