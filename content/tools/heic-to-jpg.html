HEIC to JPG converter

Turn HEIC files into JPG in a couple of taps. Drop in one file or a whole batch; each one converts on your device with its own download button.

Quick answer

To convert HEIC to JPG, choose your HEIC files above and tap Download next to each result. The conversion runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded and there's no limit on how many files you convert.

How to convert HEIC to JPG

  1. Choose or drop your HEIC files above. You can select many at once.
  2. Each file converts straight away and shows its new size.
  3. Tap Download next to each one.

Why convert HEIC to JPG

iPhones save photos as HEIC by default. It's an efficient format, but many Android phones, Windows PCs, websites and forms can't open it. When an iPhone user sends you photos as files, or you copy them from an old iPhone, converting to JPG makes them open everywhere.

HEIC photos are decoded on your device by a small open-source decoder that this page loads, so the photo itself still never leaves your phone.

Private by design

The tool runs entirely inside this browser tab. Your photo is opened, edited and saved on your own device, and it is never sent to a server, so there is nothing for anyone to keep, scan or leak. No account, no watermark and no limit on how many photos you do. It even keeps working if you switch off your internet after the page has loaded.
