Fix dark photos

Indoor parties, evening dinners and photos taken against a window often come out dark. Brightening them with a curve, instead of just turning everything up, brings back faces without washing out the rest.

Quick answer

Choose the dark photo above; Auto fix lifts the shadows immediately. Nudge Brightness, Contrast and Colour if you like, then download the full-resolution result. Everything happens on your device.

Why photos come out dark

  • Low light: the camera can't gather enough light without blur.
  • Backlight: a bright window or sky behind the subject fools the exposure.
  • Dark scenes: cameras sometimes keep night scenes dark on purpose.

Getting a natural result

  1. Start with Auto fix.
  2. Raise Brightness until faces look right; stop before the darkest areas turn grey.
  3. Add a little Contrast to bring back depth.
  4. Lift Colour slightly if skin looks pale.

Next time

  • Tap the subject's face on screen before shooting, so the camera exposes for it.
  • Use Night mode for still scenes, and hold the phone steady.
  • Turn so the light is behind you, not behind the subject.
