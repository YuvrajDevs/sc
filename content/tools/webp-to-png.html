Turn WebP files into PNG in a couple of taps. Drop in one file or a whole batch; each one converts on your device with its own download button.

Quick answer

To convert WebP to PNG, choose your WebP files above and tap Download next to each result. The conversion runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded and there's no limit on how many files you convert.

How to convert WebP to PNG

  1. Choose or drop your WebP files above. You can select many at once.
  2. Each file converts straight away and shows its new size.
  3. Tap Download next to each one.

Why convert WebP to PNG

PNG keeps transparency and sharp edges, which makes it the right choice for logos, stickers, icons and screenshots saved from websites as WebP. It's also accepted by every editor and design app. PNG files are larger than WebP, so for ordinary photos WebP to JPG gives a smaller file.
