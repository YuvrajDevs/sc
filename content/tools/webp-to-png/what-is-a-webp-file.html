What is a WebP file?

If an image you saved from a website ended in .webp and won't open where you need it, you've met WebP: a format made by Google so web pages load faster.

Quick answer

WebP is an image format from Google that makes images smaller than JPG or PNG at similar quality, so websites use it. Browsers open it, but some apps and forms don't. Convert it to PNG above (keeps transparency) or to JPG for photos.

WebP in a nutshell

  • Created by Google and released in 2010.
  • Supports both lossy (like JPG) and lossless (like PNG) compression.
  • Supports transparency and animation.
  • Files are typically noticeably smaller than JPG or PNG at similar quality.

Why your download is WebP

Websites send WebP to browsers that support it, which is almost all of them. When you save the image, you get the format the site sent, even if the page looked like it showed a normal photo.
