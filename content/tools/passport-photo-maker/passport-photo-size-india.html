Passport size photo in India

In India, "passport size" usually means 35 × 45 mm with a plain white background, and it's asked for everywhere: visa forms, ID cards, admissions and job applications.

Quick answer

An Indian passport-size photo is 35 × 45 mm with a white background, the face centred and filling most of the frame. Choose India above, fit the face in the oval, and download the single photo for online forms or the 4×6 sheet to print copies.

The standard size

  • Size: 35 × 45 mm (about 413 × 531 pixels at 300 dpi).
  • Background: plain white.
  • Face: looking straight at the camera, neutral expression, eyes open, both ears visible where possible.
  • Recent: taken within the last few months.

Requirements differ by purpose. Visa applications for other countries follow that country's rules, and passport photos for some services are taken at the office itself. Always check the official instructions for your application.

Making it at home

  1. Stand about a metre in front of a plain white wall, facing a window for even light.
  2. Have someone take the photo at eye level, with your shoulders in the frame.
  3. Choose it in the tool above, keep India selected, and fit your face in the oval.
  4. Download the photo, or the 4×6 sheet to print several copies at a photo studio.
