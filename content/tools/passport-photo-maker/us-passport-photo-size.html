US passport photo size

US passport and visa photos are a square 2 × 2 inches, with strict rules on head size and background. Make one from a photo taken at home, and print it for the price of one 4×6 print.

Quick answer

A US passport photo is 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm), with the head between 1 and 1⅜ inches from chin to top of the head, on a white or off-white background, no glasses. Choose the US size above, fit the face in the oval and download the photo or a 4×6 print sheet.

The key requirements

  • Size: 2 × 2 inches (600 × 600 pixels at 300 dpi).
  • Head size: 1 to 1⅜ inches from chin to top of head.
  • Background: plain white or off-white, no shadows.
  • No glasses, neutral expression or a natural smile, eyes open.
  • Recent: taken in the last six months.

Rules are set by the US Department of State and can change; check travel.state.gov before you apply.

Step by step

  1. Take a photo facing a window, in front of a plain white wall, at eye level.
  2. Choose it above and keep "US passport and visa (2 × 2 in)" selected.
  3. Move and resize the box until the face fills the oval: chin at the bottom, hair near the top.
  4. Download the 4×6 sheet and print it as a regular 4×6 photo, then cut out two copies.
