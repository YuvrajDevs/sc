Print passport photos on a 4×6 sheet

Photo studios charge for a set of passport photos. A standard 4×6 inch print costs a fraction of that, and it can hold four to six passport photos at the exact size.

Quick answer

Make your passport photo above, tap "Download 4×6 in print sheet", and order it as a normal 4×6 inch (10 × 15 cm) print. Choose "actual size" or no cropping at the kiosk, then cut along the thin lines.

Printing it right

  1. Make your photo in the tool above and download the print sheet.
  2. Order a 4×6 inch (10 × 15 cm) print at a kiosk, pharmacy or online service.
  3. Choose glossy or matte photo paper, and turn off any "fill" or "auto crop" option so it prints at actual size.
  4. Check one photo with a ruler before cutting the rest.

The sheet is saved at 300 dpi, so each photo prints at its exact size.

Printing at home

Home printers often scale images to fit the page. Use a 4×6 photo tray and set the scale to 100% or "actual size". Ordinary paper isn't accepted for most official photos, so use photo paper.

Taking the photo yourself also means it never sits on a studio's computer. Keep the original somewhere private, such as an encrypted vault, and see our guide on passport-size photos in India.
