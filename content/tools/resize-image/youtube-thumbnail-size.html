YouTube thumbnail size

YouTube asks for a 16:9 image of 1280 × 720 pixels and under 2 MB. Get any of those wrong and it's rejected or shown blurry.

Quick answer

Make your thumbnail 1280 × 720 pixels in 16:9, saved as JPEG or PNG under 2 MB. Crop to 16:9, resize above to 1280 × 720, then compress to under 2,000 KB if needed.

YouTube's thumbnail rules

  • Size: 1280 × 720 pixels (minimum width 640).
  • Shape: 16:9.
  • File: JPG, PNG or GIF, under 2 MB.

Making one from a photo or screenshot

  1. Open the crop tool, choose 16:9, and frame the subject with space for text.
  2. Resize it above to 1280 × 720.
  3. If it's over 2 MB, compress it to about 1,500 KB.
  4. Upload it in YouTube Studio under Details, then Thumbnail.

Thumbnails that get clicked

  • One clear subject, large and close.
  • Three to five words of text at most, readable on a phone.
  • Strong contrast, and nothing important in the bottom-right corner, where YouTube shows the video length.

If the frame includes other people, number plates or screens with personal details, blur them before publishing.
