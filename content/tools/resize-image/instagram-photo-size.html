Instagram photo size

Instagram shows everything 1080 pixels wide, and the height decides how much of the feed your photo fills. Use the right size and nothing gets cropped or blurry.

Quick answer

Post at 1080 × 1350 (4:5 portrait) for the most feed space, 1080 × 1080 for square, and 1080 × 1920 (9:16) for stories and reels. Crop to the shape first, then resize above. Everything happens on your device.

Why 4:5 wins in the feed

Portrait posts take up the most vertical space as people scroll, so they're noticed more than square or landscape ones. Most phone photos are 3:4, slightly taller than 4:5, so a gentle crop is all you need.

How to resize for Instagram

  1. Crop to the shape with the crop tool (4:5, 1:1 or 9:16).
  2. Resize above to the exact pixels in the table.
  3. Upload from your phone; Instagram won't need to crop or squash it.

Before you post

Instagram removes location data from the file itself, but the picture can still reveal a lot: house numbers, car plates, school uniforms. Blur plates and details first, and keep anything truly private out of your camera roll altogether with an encrypted vault.
