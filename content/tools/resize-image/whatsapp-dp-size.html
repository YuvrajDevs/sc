WhatsApp DP size

WhatsApp shows your profile picture as a circle cut from a square, so a tall or wide photo gets heads, hands or text chopped off. Make it square first and you choose exactly what's shown.

Quick answer

A WhatsApp DP should be square; 640 × 640 pixels or larger looks sharp everywhere. Crop your photo to 1:1 with the crop tool, or resize it above, keeping the subject in the centre because the corners are hidden by the circle.

The size and shape that works

  • Shape: square (1:1). WhatsApp crops anything else.
  • Size: 640 × 640 pixels is a good target; larger is fine.
  • Safe area: keep faces and text inside the central circle, because the corners don't show.

Fitting a full photo without cropping

If you want the whole photo visible, such as a group picture or a logo with text, it has to become square without cutting anything off. Use the crop tool with the 1:1 shape and place the box over the most important part, or accept a little border: resize the long side to 640 pixels and add the photo to a square in any editor. For most selfies, a 1:1 crop centred on the face looks best.

Step by step

  1. Open the crop tool, choose 1:1, and frame the face in the middle.
  2. Download it, then drop it in the resizer above at 640 × 640.
  3. In WhatsApp, open Settings, tap your photo, then Edit, and choose the new picture.

A privacy note

Depending on your settings, anyone with your number may see your DP. In WhatsApp's Privacy settings you can limit your profile photo to contacts, or to nobody. Photos of children are best kept off public DPs; our guide to blurring kids' faces shows a middle way.

Private by design

The resize image tool on this page runs entirely inside your browser tab. Your files are opened, processed and saved on your own device and are never sent to a server, so there's nothing for anyone to store, scan or leak. There's no sign-up, no watermark and no limit, and once the page has loaded it keeps working even if you switch off your internet.
