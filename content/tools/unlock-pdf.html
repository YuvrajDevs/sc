Remove password from a PDF

Bank statements, payslips and bills often arrive locked with your date of birth or account number. If you know the password, save a copy that opens without it.

Quick answer

To remove a password from a PDF, choose it above, type its current password and tap Unlock PDF. You get a copy without the password. This only works when you know the password; it doesn't crack or guess passwords. Nothing is uploaded.

How to unlock a PDF

  1. Choose the protected PDF.
  2. Type the password you normally use to open it.
  3. Tap Unlock PDF and download the unlocked copy.

Common statement passwords

Banks, card issuers and payroll systems often build the password from your details, for example part of your name with your date of birth, or the last digits of your account or customer ID. The email that came with the statement usually explains the format. This tool needs that password; it doesn't guess or break passwords.

Keep unlocked copies safe

A statement without a password is easier to read, for you and for anyone who finds it. Don't leave unlocked copies in Downloads, email or a shared cloud folder. Keep them in an encrypted vault such as Sealcloak, which stores documents encrypted on your phone behind your own pattern, or add a new password with protect PDF.

Private by design

Most PDF websites upload your document to their servers to process it. This one doesn't. The PDF is opened, edited and saved inside this browser tab, on your own device, using open-source PDF libraries the page loads. Contracts, bank statements, ID scans and medical letters never leave your phone or computer. No sign-up, no watermark, no file limit.
