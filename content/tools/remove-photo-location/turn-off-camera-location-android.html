Turn off camera location on Android

Your camera may be quietly saving the exact GPS spot of every photo. Turning it off takes ten seconds, and it protects every picture you take from now on.

    Quick answer

    Open your Camera app's settings and turn off the option called Location tags, Save location or GPS tag. On Samsung it's Location tags; on Pixel it's Save location. Photos you've already taken keep their location, so clean those with the tool above before sharing.

    Where to find it

    PhoneSetting
    Samsung GalaxyCamera → Settings (gear) → Location tags
    Google PixelCamera → Settings → Save location
    Xiaomi, Redmi, POCOCamera → Settings → Save location info
    OPPO, realme, OnePlusCamera → Settings → Location
    vivoCamera → Settings → Location / GPS tag

    Wording changes between versions. If you can't find it, search the Settings app for "location" and look under Camera, or remove the camera app's location permission in Settings → Apps → Camera → Permissions.

    Check it worked

    1. Take a test photo.
    2. Drop it into the tool above.
    3. If no location appears, you're done.
