Remove location from a photo on iPhone

iPhone photos record where they were taken. You can strip that each time you share, stop it at the camera, or clean any file in your browser.

    Quick answer

    On iPhone, tap Share, then Options at the top, and turn off Location before sending. To stop it for every new photo, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Camera and choose Never. You can also drop a photo in the tool above for a clean copy.

    Three ways

    1. When sharing: tap Share, then Options under the photo preview, and switch off Location.
    2. For all new photos: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Camera → Never.
    3. For any file: drop it in the tool above and download the clean copy. This also works for photos you've moved to a computer.

    HEIC photos

    iPhones save photos as HEIC by default. This tool cleans JPEG and PNG, so either share as "Most Compatible" or convert first with HEIC to JPG, which runs on your device too. You can set future photos to JPEG in Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible.

    Sending to Android users

    If you send originals to someone on Android, as a file or by email, the location goes with them unless you remove it. And if you move to Android yourself, see our guide to opening HEIC photos on Android.

    Private by design

    The remove photo location tool on this page runs entirely inside your browser tab. Your files are opened, processed and saved on your own device and are never sent to a server, so there's nothing for anyone to store, scan or leak. There's no sign-up, no watermark and no limit, and once the page has loaded it keeps working even if you switch off your internet.
