    Quick answer

    To find where a photo was taken, drop the original file in the tool above. If it has GPS data, you'll see the coordinates; paste them into Google Maps to see the spot. Photos sent through WhatsApp or downloaded from social media usually have the location removed.

    What you can find out

    • GPS coordinates, often accurate to a few metres.
    • Date and time the photo was taken.
    • Phone make and model.

    To see the place, copy the two numbers (latitude, longitude) into the search box of Google Maps or any map app.

    When there's no location

    • The camera had location tags turned off.
    • The photo was sent through WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or similar apps, which usually strip it.
    • It's a screenshot, which never has a location.
    • It was edited or saved by an app that removed the data.

    The same data that helps you find where a photo was taken lets anyone else find out where you were, including your home. Before posting or sending originals, use the tool's "Download clean copy" to remove the location, and learn how to stop your camera adding it.
