Blur a license plate

A number plate links a photo to a car, an address and an owner. Blur it before posting car photos, selling a vehicle or sharing street pictures.

Quick answer

To blur a license plate, choose the photo above, drag a box over the plate, set Pixelate or Blur to high strength, and download. Cover the whole plate, including the edges. Nothing is uploaded.

Step by step

  1. Choose the photo above.
  2. Drag a box a little larger than the plate.
  3. Choose Pixelate with a high strength; it's the easiest to judge by eye.
  4. Check the result at full size, then download.

When to blur plates

  • Selling a car online: buyers can see the car without the plate.
  • Posting road trip and parking photos.
  • Dashcam clips and street photography.
  • Photos taken outside your home, where a plate links to your address.

Don't forget the rest

Car photos often show more: a house number, a street sign, a parking permit, a reflection. Blur those too, and remove the hidden GPS location from the file before posting.

Private by design

The blur faces tool on this page runs entirely inside your browser tab. Your files are opened, processed and saved on your own device and are never sent to a server, so there's nothing for anyone to store, scan or leak. There's no sign-up, no watermark and no limit, and once the page has loaded it keeps working even if you switch off your internet.
