Blur kids' faces in photos

Plenty of parents want to share the moment without putting their children's faces online for good. Blur or pixelate faces in seconds, and decide later what your kids want public.

Quick answer

Choose the photo above, drag a box over each child's face, set Blur or Pixelate to a high strength and download. Check for school logos, names on clothing and house numbers too. The photo never leaves your phone.

Step by step

  1. Choose the photo above.
  2. Drag a box over each face, slightly bigger than the face.
  3. Set the strength high, then check the result at full size.
  4. Download and share the blurred copy.

What else gives children away

  • School uniforms, badges and logos.
  • Names on bags, bottles and sports shirts.
  • House numbers, street signs and car plates.
  • The photo's hidden location, which can point to home or school; remove it first.

Keep the originals private

The unblurred originals still matter. Family photos sync to cloud accounts and sit in galleries that anyone borrowing your phone can scroll. Keeping the ones you treasure in an encrypted vault keeps them yours. Our page on kids using your phone has more tips.

Private by design

The blur faces tool on this page runs entirely inside your browser tab. Your files are opened, processed and saved on your own device and are never sent to a server, so there's nothing for anyone to store, scan or leak. There's no sign-up, no watermark and no limit, and once the page has loaded it keeps working even if you switch off your internet.
