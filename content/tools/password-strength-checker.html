Password strength checker

Type a password to see how strong it really is, roughly how long it would take to crack, and which common patterns make it weaker than it looks.

Quick answer

Type a password above to see its strength, an estimate of how long it would take to crack, and specific weaknesses such as common words, sequences or years. The check runs in your browser, and the password is never sent or stored.

What the checker looks at

  • Length and the variety of characters: lowercase, capitals, digits and symbols.
  • Common passwords, including ones with a number or symbol tacked on.
  • Sequences like 1234, abcd and qwerty, and repeated characters.
  • Years and dates, which attackers try early.
  • Predictable patterns, like a capital letter, a word, then numbers.

Reading the result

The estimate assumes two attacks. "From a stolen password database" means an attacker with fast hardware guessing offline, the worst case after a data breach. "Guessing online" means trying passwords on a login page, which is far slower because sites limit attempts. Strong passwords hold up against both.

Private by design

The checker runs entirely in your browser. What you type isn't sent anywhere, saved or logged. Even so, it's best practice to check a password that's similar to yours rather than your real one. Need a better one? Use the password generator, and see how long a password should be.
