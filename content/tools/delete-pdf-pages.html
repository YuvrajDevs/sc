Delete pages from a PDF

Drop blank scans, duplicate pages, or pages with details you don't want to share, and send a cleaner PDF.

Quick answer

To delete pages from a PDF, choose it above, type the pages to remove (for example 2, 7-9), and tap Remove pages. You get a new PDF without them; the original isn't changed. Everything happens on your device.

How to remove pages

  1. Choose the PDF; you'll see its page count.
  2. Type the pages to remove, such as 3 or 2, 7-9.
  3. Tap Remove pages and download the edited PDF.

Share only what's needed

Documents often carry more than the recipient needs. A rental application rarely needs every page of a bank statement; a form might only need the signed page. Removing extra pages is a simple privacy habit: less information shared is less information that can leak, be forwarded or be misused.

To keep specific pages instead of deleting, use split PDF.

Private by design

Most PDF websites upload your document to their servers to process it. This one doesn't. The PDF is opened, edited and saved inside this browser tab, on your own device, using open-source PDF libraries the page loads. Contracts, bank statements, ID scans and medical letters never leave your phone or computer. No sign-up, no watermark, no file limit.

Before sharing, also remove the PDF's hidden metadata, such as author name and software.
