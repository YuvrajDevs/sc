Brighten photo

Rescue a photo taken indoors, at night or against the light. Auto fix lifts the shadows without washing out the bright parts, and you can fine-tune it by hand.

Quick answer

To brighten a dark photo, choose it above; Auto fix runs straight away. Adjust Brightness, Contrast and Colour if you like, then tap Download for a full-resolution copy. Your photo stays on your device.

How to brighten a photo

  1. Choose a photo above. Auto fix is applied straight away.
  2. Move Brightness to lift or darken the shadows, Contrast to add punch, and Colour to bring back faded colours.
  3. Tap Download. The changes are applied to the full-size photo, not the preview.

Why this looks natural

Simply adding brightness makes everything lighter, including skies and faces that were already bright, so they turn white and lose detail. This tool brightens with a curve instead: dark areas are lifted a lot, mid-tones a little, and highlights hardly at all. That's how phone editors and photo apps fix underexposed pictures, and it keeps the photo looking real.

Getting the best result

  • Very dark night photos carry noise; brighten moderately and add a little contrast rather than pushing brightness to the top.
  • Photos taken against a window: brighten until the face looks right and accept that the window stays bright.
  • Old scanned prints: raise Colour a touch to undo fading.

Private by design

The tool runs entirely inside this browser tab. Your photo is opened, edited and saved on your own device, and it is never sent to a server, so there is nothing for anyone to keep, scan or leak. No account, no watermark and no limit on how many photos you do. It even keeps working if you switch off your internet after the page has loaded.
