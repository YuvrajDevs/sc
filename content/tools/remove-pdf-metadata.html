Remove PDF metadata

PDFs quietly carry details about who made them: your name as author, your organisation, the software used, and when it was created and edited. See them here, and remove them.

Quick answer

To remove PDF metadata, choose the PDF above. You'll see its hidden properties, such as author, title and software. Tap Remove metadata to download a clean copy without them. The PDF is cleaned on your device and never uploaded.

What a PDF can reveal

  • Author: often your full name or your computer's user name.
  • Title and subject: sometimes an old draft name or internal reference.
  • Creator and producer: the app and version that made it.
  • Created and modified dates, to the second.
  • XMP metadata: a second, hidden copy of these details, plus editing history in some files.

When to clean a PDF

  • Sending a CV, a complaint or a tip where your name or employer shouldn't appear in the file.
  • Publishing documents online.
  • Sharing forms or letters drafted on a work computer.

Steps

  1. Choose the PDF and review what it contains.
  2. Tap Remove metadata.
  3. Download the clean copy and share that one.

Private by design

Most PDF websites upload your document to their servers to process it. This one doesn't. The PDF is opened, edited and saved inside this browser tab, on your own device, using open-source PDF libraries the page loads. Contracts, bank statements, ID scans and medical letters never leave your phone or computer. No sign-up, no watermark, no file limit.

Photos carry hidden data too: remove the location from photos before sharing them.
