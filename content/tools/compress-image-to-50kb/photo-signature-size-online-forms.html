Photo and signature size for online forms

Online application forms are strict: a photo between 20 and 50 KB, a signature between 10 and 20 KB, exact dimensions, JPEG only. Here's how to get both files right on the first attempt, from your phone.

Quick answer

Most online forms want a recent passport-style photo of about 20 to 50 KB and a signature of about 10 to 20 KB, both as JPEG. Crop each tightly, then compress it with the tool above; the target is set to 50 KB, and you can type 20 for a signature. Always check the exact rules in the form's notification.

These are common ranges, not rules. Each exam or employer publishes its own, sometimes with pixel dimensions such as 200 × 230 for the photo and 140 × 60 for the signature. If pixel sizes are given, resize first, then compress.

Step by step

  1. Take the photo against a plain light wall in daylight, face centred, shoulders visible.
  2. Crop it to head and shoulders with the crop tool, or make a proper passport-size photo.
  3. Compress it above to 45 KB so it lands safely inside a 20 to 50 KB range.
  4. Sign on white paper with a black or blue pen, photograph it, crop right to the ink, and compress it to 18 KB.

Common rejections and fixes

  • "File size too large": type a lower target, such as 40, and compress again.
  • "File size too small": forms sometimes reject tiny files. Raise the target or resize to the stated dimensions first.
  • "Invalid format": make sure the format is JPEG; iPhone HEIC photos are converted automatically.
  • Blurry signature: crop closer and use brighter, even light.

Your ID photos and signature are exactly what you shouldn't upload to random converter sites. This tool works on your device only. Keep the originals somewhere private afterwards; that's what a vault is for.
