PNG to JPG converter

Turn PNG files into JPG in a couple of taps. Drop in one file or a whole batch; each one converts on your device with its own download button.

Quick answer

To convert PNG to JPG, choose your PNG files above and tap Download next to each result. The conversion runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded and there's no limit on how many files you convert.

How to convert PNG to JPG

  1. Choose or drop your PNG files above. You can select many at once.
  2. Each file converts straight away and shows its new size.
  3. Tap Download next to each one.

Why convert PNG to JPG

PNG files can be very large, especially screenshots and photos saved as PNG. Converting to JPG usually makes them several times smaller, which helps with email, forms that ask for JPG, and storage. Transparent areas become white, because JPG has no transparency.
