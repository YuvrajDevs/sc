Open HEIC photos on Android

HEIC is the format iPhones use for photos. Newer Android phones and Google Photos can often display it, but many apps, older phones and websites can't. Converting to JPG makes it work everywhere.

Quick answer

Try opening the HEIC file in Google Photos first; recent Android versions can display it. If it won't open, or an app or website refuses it, choose the files in the converter above and download them as JPG. The conversion happens on your phone.

Why HEIC causes problems

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) stores photos at roughly half the size of JPG at similar quality, which is why iPhones use it. Support on Android depends on the phone's Android version and the app. Gallery apps, messaging apps, editors and upload forms often don't accept it.

Convert in three steps

  1. Save the HEIC files to your phone, for example from email or Files.
  2. Choose them in the converter above. They're decoded on your device by a small library this page loads.
  3. Download the JPGs. They open in any app.

Ask the sender to send JPG

iPhone users can share photos as JPG automatically: on the iPhone, Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible. Photos sent through most messaging apps are converted anyway. If they include the location, remove it before you pass them on.

Private by design

The HEIC to JPG tool on this page runs entirely inside your browser tab. Your files are opened, processed and saved on your own device and are never sent to a server, so there's nothing for anyone to store, scan or leak. There's no sign-up, no watermark and no limit, and once the page has loaded it keeps working even if you switch off your internet.
