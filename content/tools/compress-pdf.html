Compress PDF

Scanned documents and photo-heavy PDFs can be enormous. Compress them so they fit email attachments and upload limits on forms and portals.

Quick answer

To compress a PDF, choose it above, keep Recommended compression (or Strong for smaller files), and tap Compress PDF. You'll see the new size; if the PDF is already compact, the original is kept. Everything happens on your device.

How it works

The tool redraws each page as a well-compressed image and builds a new, smaller PDF. That's exactly what shrinks scans and photo-heavy documents the most, often by 50 to 90%. The trade-off is that text in the compressed copy can't be selected or searched, so keep the original for editing.

Choose a level

  • Recommended: good balance, readable on any screen and printable.
  • Strong: smallest files, fine for viewing on screens.
  • Light: sharper pages, smaller savings.

Steps

  1. Choose the PDF.
  2. Pick a compression level.
  3. Tap Compress PDF, check the new size, and download.

Private by design

Most PDF websites upload your document to their servers to process it. This one doesn't. The PDF is opened, edited and saved inside this browser tab, on your own device, using open-source PDF libraries the page loads. Contracts, bank statements, ID scans and medical letters never leave your phone or computer. No sign-up, no watermark, no file limit.

If a portal needs a specific size, try Strong, or remove pages you don't need first.
