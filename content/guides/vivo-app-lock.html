Vivo app lock: how to lock apps and the gallery on a vivo phone

Someone keeps picking up your phone, or you just don't want WhatsApp and your photos open to whoever borrows it. Vivo app lock is built into every recent vivo and iQOO phone, so you don't need to install anything. This guide shows where vivo hides the setting, how to lock apps and Albums step by step, and what the lock does and doesn't protect.

Quick answer

On a vivo phone, app lock is called Privacy and app encryption. Open Settings, then Fingerprint, face and password (Password & security on some newer builds), then Privacy and app encryption. Set a privacy password and security questions, open App encryption, and turn on the switch next to each app you want to lock, including Albums. The app then asks for the privacy password or your fingerprint each time it opens.

Where to find app lock in vivo settings

Vivo has moved and renamed this menu a few times, which is why so many people search for it. The feature itself has stayed the same: a separate privacy password that guards the apps you pick.

  • Funtouch OS 3.0 and newer: vivo's support pages put it in Settings, in the Fingerprint, face and password section, under Privacy and app encryption.
  • Recent Funtouch OS and OriginOS builds: the parent section may be called Password & security instead, and the inner option may say App lock or App encryption.
  • Old phones on Funtouch OS 2.6 or earlier: it was in the i Manager app, under App manager, then App lock.

If the names on your screen don't match, use the search bar at the top of Settings and type "app encryption" or "privacy password". That works on every version we've seen and saves hunting through menus.

Step-by-step: how to app lock in vivo mobile

  1. Open Settings and go to Privacy and app encryption. Use the path above or search for it. The first time, vivo asks you to create a password.
  2. Set a privacy password and security questions. This password is separate from your screen lock. Choose something different, and pick security questions whose answers you'll still know in two years. You'll need them if you forget the password.
  3. Tap App encryption. You'll see a list of installed apps with a switch next to each one.
  4. Turn on the switch for each app you want to lock. Common choices are WhatsApp, Messages, Albums, File Manager, your email app and any banking app.
  5. Allow fingerprint or face if you want. Many vivo phones let you open locked apps with your fingerprint instead of typing the password every time. The privacy password still works as a fallback.
  6. Check App lock time. vivo notes that an encrypted app doesn't ask again if you reopen it within about a minute. On Funtouch OS 4.5 and newer, App lock time in the same menu lets you change that.
  7. Test it. Close a locked app fully, then open it again. It should ask for the password or fingerprint.

To turn it all off later, vivo's help says to open the same menu and tap Disable privacy password. Note that this also switches off File safe, so move anything out of File safe first.

How to lock the gallery on vivo

The gallery app on vivo phones is called Albums. You can lock it like any other app: turn on its switch under App encryption. Anyone who opens Albums then has to enter the privacy password.

There's a catch. vivo's own FAQ says the albums inside Albums can't be locked one by one. It's all or nothing. If you only want to protect a handful of photos, you have two built-in options and one outside one:

  • File safe: in File Manager, File safe (Private safe on some models) uses the same privacy password and moves files out of the normal gallery.
  • Lock both Albums and File Manager: if you lock only Albums, the same photos are often still visible through File Manager, so lock both.
  • An encrypted vault app: covered further down, for when you want the photos protected even outside vivo's own apps.

What vivo app lock doesn't protect (the part most guides skip)

App lock is a door in front of an app. It's useful, and for stopping a friend or a child from opening WhatsApp it's exactly the right tool. But it isn't a safe around your data, and it helps to know where the gaps are.

  • The data underneath isn't locked. If you lock Albums, your photos still sit in the phone's normal storage. File Manager, any app with photo access, a computer on USB, or a cloud backup can still show them.
  • Notifications can leak content. vivo's FAQ lists "Relevant data still can be seen after app locked" as a known question. Message previews on the lock screen or in the notification shade can show text from a locked chat app. Turn off previews for those apps in notification settings.
  • The one-minute window. Hand the phone over right after you've used a locked app, and it may open without asking.
  • Some apps can't be locked. vivo's help covers this too. Older Funtouch versions, for example, couldn't encrypt the Settings app.
  • A known password opens everything. One privacy password guards every locked app and File safe. If someone learns it, they get all of them at once.

None of this makes vivo app lock bad. It means it's the right tool for apps and the wrong one on its own for private photos or documents. Protecting photos and files beyond the app lock

If your worry is the photos themselves, Sealcloak is one option built for that job. It's an offline vault for photos, videos, documents, audio, notes and scans on Android 8.0 or newer, and it runs on vivo and iQOO phones like any other Android app.

  • No account and no internet. There's no sign-up, email or phone number, and the app has no internet permission, so it can't upload anything.
  • Real encryption. Every file is encrypted on the phone with AES-256-GCM. A USB copy or a file browser only sees scrambled data. The security page explains how the keys work.
  • Its own lock. The vault opens with a pattern on a 5x5 grid of at least six dots, separate from your vivo privacy password, with optional fingerprint or face unlock.
  • A record of snooping. Optional intruder photos take a front-camera picture from the third wrong pattern in a row, which you can view after unlocking.
  • A way back in. Each vault has a 9-word recovery phrase if you forget the pattern. Nobody else holds a copy, so lose both and the vault can't be opened by anyone.

To be clear about the limits: Sealcloak can't lock or hide other apps, so keep using vivo app lock for WhatsApp and Messages. And it doesn't reach into Albums by itself. You add the photos you want to protect, it offers to keep the originals, and you delete them from the gallery yourself. The two together cover both jobs: vivo app lock for apps, a vault for files.

Forgot your vivo app lock password?

  1. Open a locked app or the Privacy and app encryption menu. When it asks for the privacy password, look for Forgot password.
  2. Answer your security questions. These are the questions you chose during setup. Some versions also let you verify with your vivo account.
  3. Set a new privacy password. Then check your locked apps and File safe still look right.

If you can't answer the questions, contact a vivo service centre. Don't clear the data of locked apps or install "unlock" tools from video tutorials: clearing data wipes chats and settings, and unknown APKs are a bigger risk than a forgotten password. The same advice applies on other brands; our Xiaomi App Lock forgot password guide walks through a similar problem on Redmi and POCO.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Using your screen lock as the privacy password. Anyone who's watched you unlock the phone then has both.
  • Locking Albums but not File Manager. The photos are usually still one tap away.
  • Leaving message previews on. A locked chat app can still show messages in notifications.
  • Skipping the security questions. They're your only built-in way back if you forget the password.
  • Assuming locked means private everywhere. Cloud backups such as Google Photos still show your pictures wherever that account is signed in.

If you'd rather an app didn't show on the home screen at all, see how to hide apps on Android, and combine hiding with a lock.

Bottom line

Vivo app lock lives in Settings under Privacy and app encryption. Set a privacy password and security questions, open App encryption and switch on the apps you want locked, including Albums and File Manager. It's quick, free and good at stopping someone from opening your apps on an unlocked phone. It doesn't encrypt your photos or stop other apps and computers from seeing them, so for truly private pictures and documents, use File safe or an encrypted offline vault alongside it.
