Motorola app lock: how to lock apps on a Moto phone

You want WhatsApp, your photos or your banking app to stay closed when someone borrows your Moto. If you've looked for a Motorola app lock switch next to each app, you probably didn't find one. Motorola does it differently, with a locked Secure folder. This guide shows where the setting lives, how to use it step by step, and what it does and doesn't protect.

Quick answer

Motorola's built-in app lock is Secure folder, part of Moto Secure on phones with Android 13 or newer. Open Settings, then Security & privacy, then More security & privacy, and tap Secure folder. Choose a pattern, PIN or password (fingerprint is optional), then tap the plus icon inside the folder and add the apps you want locked. Delete the outside copy of an app if it should only exist behind the lock.

Where app lock is on a Motorola phone

Samsung, vivo and Xiaomi phones have an app lock menu where you flip a switch for each app. Most Motorola phones don't. Instead, Motorola gives you Secure folder: a locked area with its own copy of the apps you put in it. Motorola's own help describes it as a way to "protect apps in a secure folder", and its UK blog from May 2025 calls it a PIN-protected folder for hiding apps or files.

You can reach it two ways:

  • Through Settings: Settings, then Security & privacy, then More security & privacy, then Secure folder. On some older builds the middle step is called More security settings.
  • Through the Moto Secure app: open Moto Secure from the app tray and tap Secure folder.

Motorola says Moto Secure is on its phones running Android 13 or newer. If the names on your screen don't match, type "secure folder" into the search bar at the top of Settings. Some recent Moto Secure updates list a per-app App lock option in their release notes, but Motorola's help pages don't describe it yet, so treat it as a bonus if your phone shows it rather than something every Moto has.

Step-by-step: how to app lock in a Motorola phone

  1. Open Settings, then Security & privacy, then More security & privacy. Or open the Moto Secure app.
  2. Tap Secure folder. The first time, it walks you through a short setup. Some carrier versions, such as AT&T's guide for the moto g, also ask you to sign in with a Google account here.
  3. Unlock your phone and choose a lock type. Motorola offers a pattern, a PIN of 4 to 16 digits, or a password of 4 to 16 characters. Motorola notes that longer PINs are more secure. Pick something different from your screen lock.
  4. Turn on fingerprint if you want. It's quicker for daily use, and the pattern, PIN or password still works as a fallback.
  5. Open the folder and tap the plus icon, then Add apps. Choose apps already on your phone, or install new ones from Google Play inside the folder.
  6. Sign in again inside the folder. The folder's copy of an app is separate, so log in to WhatsApp, Instagram or your bank app again there.
  7. Delete the outside copy. If you add WhatsApp to the folder but keep the normal WhatsApp too, anyone can still open the normal one. Motorola's help says to delete the outside version if you want an app only in the folder.
  8. Test it. Close the folder, open it again from the app tray, and check that it asks for your lock.

Hide the folder and quiet its notifications

A locked folder still tells people there's something locked. Motorola gives you two ways to make it less obvious, both in the folder's own menu:

  • Disguised icons: change the folder's icon and name so it looks like something ordinary.
  • Stealth mode: hide the folder from the app tray, from Moto Secure, or both. You can still open it through Settings.

Then check notifications. In the folder's menu, go to More settings, then Notification Manager. You can show the app icon, name and message content, only the icon and name, or nothing at all from apps in the folder. If you've locked a chat app, choose one of the last two. Otherwise the message text can still pop up on the lock screen. For more ways to keep an app off the home screen, see how to hide apps on Android.

What Motorola app lock doesn't protect (the part most guides skip)

Secure folder is good at one job: stopping someone from opening the apps inside it on your unlocked phone. It's worth knowing where that job ends.

  • Your photos aren't in the folder unless you put them there. Locking an app doesn't move your pictures. Photos in the normal gallery stay visible to Google Photos, Files, any app with photo access, and a computer on USB.
  • Cloud copies stay visible. If Google Photos backup is on, your pictures also show up on any device signed in to that Google account.
  • There's no forgotten-lock reset. Motorola is clear about this: if you forget the folder's lock, you delete the folder and set it up again, and that deletes all apps and files inside. Make a note of the lock somewhere safe.
  • The outside copy is still open. Moving an app in doesn't remove the original. Forget to delete it and the lock protects nothing.
  • Notifications can leak. Until you change Notification Manager, a locked chat app can still show messages.

None of this makes Secure folder a bad choice. It means it's built for apps, and private photos and documents need a place of their own. Our guide on how to hide photos on Android explains the difference between hiding, locking and encrypting.

Locking photos and files on a Moto, not just apps

Most recent Motorola phones use Google Photos as the gallery. Its Locked Folder is the free built-in option: photos you move there disappear from the main grid and from other apps, and open with your screen lock. That's fine for keeping a few pictures out of sight, but anyone who knows your phone's PIN can open it too. Our gallery lock guide covers it across brands.

If you want the files themselves protected with a separate lock, Sealcloak is one option built for that. It's an offline vault for photos, videos, documents, audio, notes and scans, and it runs on Moto phones with Android 8.0 or newer.

  • No account and no internet. There's no sign-up, email or phone number. The app has no internet permission, so it can't upload anything.
  • Real encryption. Every file is encrypted on the phone with AES-256-GCM. A file browser or USB copy only sees scrambled data. The security page explains how the keys work.
  • Its own lock. The vault opens with a pattern on a 5x5 grid of at least six dots, separate from your phone and Secure folder locks, with optional fingerprint or face unlock.
  • A way back in. Unlike Secure folder, each vault has a 9-word recovery phrase if you forget the pattern. Nobody else holds a copy, so if you lose both, no one can open the vault.
  • A record of snooping. Optional intruder photos take a front-camera picture from the third wrong pattern in a row.

The honest limits: Sealcloak can't lock or hide other apps, so keep Secure folder for WhatsApp and the like. It also doesn't reach into your gallery on its own. You add the photos you want to protect, it offers to keep the originals, and you delete them from the gallery yourself. Secure folder for apps and a vault for files cover both jobs.

Forgot your Motorola secure folder lock?

There's no reset question or account recovery for Secure folder. Motorola's help gives one route:

  1. Go to Settings, then Security & privacy, then More security & privacy, then Secure folder.
  2. Tap Delete secure folder.
  3. Unlock your phone with your normal screen lock or fingerprint.
  4. Confirm. Everything in the folder is deleted. Reinstall any app that only existed inside it, then set up a new folder.

Don't install "unlock" tools from video tutorials. They can't break the lock and unknown APKs are a bigger risk than a lost folder. The same problem on other brands is covered in our Xiaomi App Lock forgot password guide.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Using your screen lock for the folder. Anyone who has watched you unlock the phone then has both.
  • Leaving the original app outside. Delete it, or the lock only guards a second copy.
  • Keeping full notification previews. Switch Notification Manager to icon and name only, or nothing.
  • Storing the only copy of important files in the folder. Forgetting the lock means deleting them, so keep a backup elsewhere.
  • Assuming a locked app means locked photos. Move private pictures into a locked or encrypted place as well.

Bottom line

Motorola app lock means Secure folder in Moto Secure. Find it in Settings under Security & privacy, then More security & privacy. Choose a lock that differs from your screen lock, add the apps, delete their outside copies, and turn down notification previews. Use Disguised icons or Stealth mode if you don't want the folder noticed. It's free and does a good job for apps, but it has no recovery and doesn't touch photos left in your gallery. For those, use Google Photos Locked Folder or an encrypted offline vault alongside it.
