Xiaomi App Lock forgot password: how to reset it without losing your data

You locked WhatsApp, Gallery or a banking app with Xiaomi's App Lock, and now the password won't come back to you. If you forgot your Xiaomi App Lock password, there is an official way back on Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones, but it only works if one box was ticked on the day you set it up. This guide shows the steps, what happens if that box wasn't ticked, and how to avoid ending up here again.

Quick answer

Go to Settings, then Apps, then App lock, and tap Forgot password on the password screen. If you linked a Xiaomi Account when you turned App Lock on, entering that account's password lets you set a new App Lock password. If no Xiaomi Account was linked, Xiaomi says the only remaining option is a factory reset, so back up what you can before you do anything drastic.

Before you try anything: what not to do

Being locked out of your own apps is stressful, and the quick fixes you'll find in videos can make things worse.

  • Don't keep hammering in guesses. A handful of slow, careful tries with passwords you've used before is reasonable. Fifty fast ones rarely help, and lock screens commonly add waits after repeated failures.
  • Don't clear the Security app's data or uninstall locked apps. Clearing a locked app's data or uninstalling it wipes that app's chats, settings and downloads. That trades a forgotten password for real data loss.
  • Don't install "App Lock remover" or unlock tools. Many search results for this problem push paid PC software or APKs from unknown sites. Some want you to switch off security settings or grant full device access. Xiaomi's own reset is free and built in, so try it first.
  • Don't factory reset yet. That really is the last step, and the section below explains what to save before it.

Step-by-step: reset a forgotten Xiaomi App Lock password

These steps follow Xiaomi's own support pages for recent phones such as the Xiaomi 14T, Xiaomi 14T Pro and Redmi 13C 5G. Older MIUI phones and newer HyperOS phones use the same path; labels can differ a little by model and region.

  1. Open Settings and tap Apps. On some phones you may need to scroll down to find it.
  2. Tap App lock. You'll be asked for the App Lock password, pattern or PIN, which is the one you've forgotten.
  3. Tap Forgot password on that password screen. On some models the link sits at the bottom of the screen, and on some it shows after a wrong attempt.
  4. Verify with your Xiaomi Account. If a Xiaomi Account was linked to App Lock, enter that account's password. Some older models offer a choice of verifying by email, phone number or Xiaomi Account ID instead.
  5. Set a new App Lock password. Choose something you'll remember that is different from your screen lock, then check which apps are still locked. Xiaomi warns that on some models this reset clears App Lock's own settings, including the list of locked apps, so you may have to pick them again.
  6. Confirm the account link. While you're in App Lock settings, check that your Xiaomi Account is still linked, so the same reset works next time.

If the Forgot password link doesn't appear, or it asks for an account you never added, the phone has no account to verify you with. Skip to the section on resetting without a Xiaomi Account.

If you forgot the Xiaomi Account password as well

This is common: the App Lock reset asks for the Xiaomi Account password, and that's another password you haven't typed in years. Fix the account first.

  1. On any device, open Xiaomi's account sign-in page and choose Forgot password.
  2. Enter the phone number or email address linked to your Xiaomi Account.
  3. Enter the verification code Xiaomi sends, then choose a new account password.
  4. Go back to Settings, Apps, App lock, tap Forgot password, and use the new account password.

If you no longer have the phone number or email on the account, Xiaomi's customer support is the only route. Be wary of anyone else offering to unlock a Xiaomi Account for a fee.

Redmi or POCO App Lock forgot password with no Xiaomi Account

Xiaomi's support pages are clear: if you didn't add a Xiaomi Account when you turned App Lock on, you can only reset the password after a factory data reset. You also can't add the account now, because changing App Lock settings needs the password you've lost.

A factory reset deletes everything stored only on the phone. Before you do it, save what you can reach without the forgotten password:

  • Photos and videos. If Google Photos backup was on, check photos.google.com from a computer to see what's already safe. Otherwise, connect the phone to a computer and copy the DCIM and Pictures folders. Locking the Gallery app with App Lock doesn't stop file transfer over USB once the phone itself is unlocked.
  • WhatsApp and other chats. If WhatsApp itself is locked, you can't make a fresh backup. Check whether an earlier chat backup exists in your Google account before resetting.
  • Contacts and accounts. Make sure contacts are synced to your Google or Xiaomi account and that you know the password of every account you'll need to sign back in to, including Google, because the phone will ask for it after the reset.
  • Two-factor apps. If you use an authenticator app, move its codes or note the backup codes first. Losing these is often worse than losing the App Lock password.

Then reset from Settings. The menu name varies between MIUI and HyperOS versions, so the quickest way is to type "factory reset" into the search bar at the top of Settings and follow the wording on your screen.

Why App Lock resets so easily, and what that tells you

It's worth stepping back. App Lock is a door in front of an app, not a safe around its data. That's why Xiaomi can let you back in with your account password: the app data was never encrypted with your App Lock code in the first place.

This is fine for what App Lock is for: stopping a friend or child from opening your messages while they borrow the phone. But it has limits you should know about if you're using it to protect private photos:

  • Anyone who knows your Xiaomi Account password can reset it.
  • Locking the Gallery app doesn't lock the photos. Other apps with storage access, a file manager, or a computer on USB can still see them.
  • Photos backed up to Google Photos or another cloud are visible wherever that account is signed in.

If what you really want to protect is a set of photos, videos or documents, App Lock is the wrong tool on its own. Our guides on gallery lock on Android and how to hide photos on Android compare the options that move files out of the gallery entirely.

How to avoid being locked out again

  • Link your Xiaomi Account to App Lock today. Open App lock settings and check that an account is added. It's the difference between a two-minute reset and a factory reset.
  • Write the Xiaomi Account password down somewhere safe off the phone, along with the recovery phone number or email it uses.
  • Use a code that isn't your screen lock, but one you'll actually remember. A pattern or PIN you use daily is easier to keep than a password you type once a month.
  • Test the reset once. Tap Forgot password while you still remember everything, just to see that the account option appears.
  • Keep backups of what matters. A copy of your photos on a computer or a backup of your vault in a folder you control protects you from forgotten passwords, lost phones and broken screens at once.

If you want apps out of sight rather than just locked, see how to hide apps on Android, which explains what hiding an app really does and doesn't hide.

Bottom line

For a Xiaomi App Lock forgot password problem, the fix is Settings, Apps, App lock, Forgot password, then your Xiaomi Account password. That works on Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones as long as an account was linked when App Lock was turned on. If it wasn't, Xiaomi's only answer is a factory reset, so back up everything you can reach first and avoid paid unlock tools. Once you're back in, link the account and write the password down. And if it's private photos you were protecting, consider moving them into an encrypted vault instead of relying on a lock in front of the Gallery.
