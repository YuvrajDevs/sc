What the app is

Sealcloak is an encrypted file vault for Android. It encrypts your photos, videos, documents, audio, notes and scans on your phone, with keys that come from your pattern. We do not run servers that store your files, pattern or keys, and the app cannot connect to the internet. Backups are files you create and keep yourself.

Your responsibilities

  • Remember your pattern. It is what unlocks each vault.
  • Save your recovery phrase. At setup you receive a recovery phrase for each vault. Store it somewhere safe and private, ideally on paper. It is the way back in if you forget your pattern.
  • Keep your own backups. Use the app’s encrypted backup, and keep the file, and its phrase or passphrase, somewhere safe. Removing the app, clearing its data, or losing or resetting your phone removes the vaults stored on it.

No data recovery

Because the encryption happens on your phone, we never hold a key that can open your vault. If you lose both your pattern and your recovery phrase, and have no unlocked copy or usable backup, nobody can open that vault, including us. Support cannot bypass this, and there is no account to reset.

Coerce, and erasing data

Coerce is a second vault with its own pattern, meant for situations where you are forced to unlock the app. By default it is a harmless decoy and erases nothing. You can separately choose to let it erase your other vaults when it is opened, and you can set the app to erase everything after a number of wrong patterns in a row. Both are off by default and need you to type the word PERMIT to turn them on. Erasing is permanent and cannot be undone, and it may make a vault impossible to open on that phone. Backups and copies you made yourself are not touched.
