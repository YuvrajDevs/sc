Hidden vaults: separate vaults, each with its own pattern

One vault for everyday private things, another for what really matters. Each opens with its own pattern, and none of them appears in any list.

In short

Sealcloak can hold several vaults. Each has its own pattern, recovery phrase and files. You open one by drawing its pattern, and nothing on screen says how many vaults exist. The free plan includes up to 3; Pro removes the limit.

How it works

At the lock screen, you just draw a pattern. Whichever vault that pattern belongs to opens. There's no vault picker and no list, so someone who sees one vault has no way to tell whether others exist.

Each vault stands alone

  • Its own pattern, which must differ from every other pattern.
  • Its own 9-word recovery phrase.
  • Its own files, folders and name.
  • Its own settings, such as fingerprint unlock and intruder photos.

Opening one vault never reveals another one's files or name.

Adding a vault

  1. In Settings, under More vaults, tap Add a vault.
  2. Draw your current pattern to confirm it's you.
  3. Draw the new vault's pattern twice.
  4. Write down its own recovery phrase.
  5. Lock the app and draw the new pattern to open it.

When to use more than one

Common setups: one vault for documents (IDs, bank papers) and another for personal photos, or a separate vault for things a partner or family member might ask to see. For the specific case of being forced to unlock, use Coerce, which is designed for it.
