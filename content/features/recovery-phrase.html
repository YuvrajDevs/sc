Vault recovery phrase: get back in if you forget your pattern

Strong encryption means there's no back door. Your recovery phrase is the way back in that only you hold.

In short

When you set up a vault, Sealcloak shows a 9-word recovery phrase and checks you've written it down. If you forget your pattern, tap Forgot your pattern?, enter the phrase and draw a new one. The phrase also unlocks backups. Without the pattern or the phrase, nobody can open the vault, including us.

Recovering a forgotten pattern

  1. On the pattern screen, tap Forgot your pattern?
  2. Enter your 9 recovery words.
  3. Draw a new pattern, then draw it again.
  4. Your vault opens with all its files.

Why there's no 'reset by email'

A reset link needs a company that holds a key to your files. Sealcloak has no account and no server, and your files are encrypted on your phone. That's what keeps them private, and it's also why the phrase matters so much.

Random words or your own sentence

At setup, Sealcloak gives you 9 random words. You can choose to write your own sentence of six words or more instead. Random words are harder to guess; a sentence of your own can be easier to remember. Either way, it's the only way back in.

Where to keep it

  • On paper, somewhere safe at home.
  • Not in a note, photo, screenshot or message on the same phone.
  • One phrase per vault: label each one so you know which is which.

It also protects your backups

When you export a backup, the recommended choice locks it with your recovery phrase. Entering it also checks you still have the right words.
