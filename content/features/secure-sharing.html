Features · Secure sharing

Share files securely as a passphrase-locked package

Sometimes a private file needs to reach one other person. Sealcloak packs it into an encrypted file that only the passphrase opens.

In short

Select files or a folder in your vault and choose Share securely. Sealcloak makes one encrypted package locked with a passphrase you choose. Send the file with any app you like, and give the passphrase in a separate message. The other person imports it into Sealcloak with the passphrase.

Sending

  1. Press and hold a file, or select several, or open a folder and choose Share this folder.
  2. Choose Share securely and set a passphrase.
  3. Send the package with WhatsApp, email, Drive or any other app.
  4. Give the passphrase another way: in person, by call or in a different app.

Receiving

The other person opens Sealcloak, chooses Import backup in Settings, picks the package and enters the passphrase. The files land in their vault, encrypted.

Good uses

Sending ID scans to a family member, sharing medical documents with someone you trust, or moving a few files from your vault to another phone without making a full backup.

Why separate channels matter

Anyone with both the file and the passphrase can open it. Sending them together in one chat means one leak exposes both. Splitting them across channels is what makes the package safe in transit. For other ways to share privately, see vault apps without an account.
