Shared family tablet: keeping everyone's photos private

A family tablet is great for films, homework and video calls. It's also where everyone's photos, downloads and logins end up in one pile. A few settings let the whole family share the device without sharing everything on it.

What to do now

If the tablet supports multiple users, give each adult their own profile and children a kids mode or child profile. If not, keep private photos in a vault app with its own lock, check that nobody's photos are syncing into a shared Google account, and pin the app children are using.

Set up separate profiles

  1. Open Settings and search for "Multiple users" or "Users".
  2. Add a user for each adult, each with their own Google account if they want one.
  3. Add a restricted or child profile for children, if offered, or use the tablet's kids mode.
  4. Set a screen lock on each adult profile.

Each profile has its own apps, gallery and downloads. Switch between them from the quick settings panel.

If there's only one profile

  • Check photo backup. If Google Photos is signed in to one person's account, everyone's camera photos may back up into it. Turn backup off or use separate accounts.
  • Mind downloads and screenshots, which land in shared folders.
  • Sign out of personal accounts after using email or social apps.
  • Keep private files in a vault.

A vault for each person

On a single-profile device, a vault app is the simplest way for each person to keep their own things. Sealcloak allows up to three vaults, each with its own pattern, and nothing in the app shows how many exist, so nobody sees anyone else's. Every file is encrypted, and the app has no internet permission, so nothing ends up in a shared cloud. Each vault has its own recovery phrase, so one person forgetting their pattern doesn't affect the others.

For the children

App pinning keeps a child in the cartoon or game you chose until you unlock the tablet. Our page on kids using your phone covers pinning, purchase locks and kids modes step by step.

Video calls and casting

Family tablets are often used for video calls and casting to the TV, which is when private things appear by accident: a photo widget on the home screen, a notification preview, a gallery left open in recent apps. Before a call or cast, close the gallery and chats, turn on Do Not Disturb, and turn off photo memories and widgets on the home screen. Inside Sealcloak, screenshots and screen recording are blocked, so its contents can't appear in a screen share either.

When the tablet changes hands

If you sell or pass the tablet on, back up each person's photos and vaults, remove accounts and factory reset it. See selling an old phone; the same steps apply to tablets.

Bottom line

Separate profiles where you can, a kids mode for children, and an encrypted vault per person where you can't. Then the family tablet stays shared, and everyone's private things stay theirs. See all of Sealcloak's features.
