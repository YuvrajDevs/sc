What it does

Hide your files

Photos, videos, documents, audio, notes and scans move into the vault and out of your gallery.

Protect them

Every file is encrypted on your phone. Pattern, fingerprint and intruder photos guard the door.

Never lose them

A recovery phrase and encrypted backups bring everything back, even on a new phone.

Offline by design

Your files never leave your phone.

Most vault apps want an account and sync to their cloud. Sealcloak has no account, no cloud and no internet permission at all, so there is nothing to hack on a server, and nothing to leak.

  • Encrypted with AES-256 before anything is saved
  • Your pattern is the key. We never see it and can’t reset it.
  • Screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside the app

Features

Small app. Serious protection.

Pattern lock

A 5×5 grid gives far more possible patterns than the usual 3×3.

Separate vaults

Up to three vaults free, each with its own pattern. One pattern never reveals another vault.

Disguise as another app PRO

Give Sealcloak any icon and name, so it looks like an ordinary app on your home screen.

Coerce

A harmless second vault with its own pattern, for when someone forces you to unlock.

Intruder photos

After a wrong pattern, the front camera quietly takes a photo for you to see later.

Encrypted backups

Back up by hand or on a weekly or monthly schedule, to any folder you choose.
